About Me

Engineering in the field and at the system level.

My work combines practical deployment and troubleshooting with an electrical engineering foundation in embedded systems, control, electronics, and signal processing.

I am an Electrical Engineering graduate from the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), currently working as a Junior IoT Engineer at Smart Tech One. I support IoT deployments across smart-city infrastructure, including gateways, environmental and water-quality instruments, smart lighting, and monitoring devices.

I enjoy solving the practical problems between a sensor and the platform that uses its data: device configuration, communication checks, field troubleshooting, and reliable telemetry. My academic projects also gave me experience with microcontrollers, feedback control, power electronics, digital communications, and MATLAB-based signal processing.
